🍬 Candy Face

Tilt your head slightly toward a side to send the candy that way. Brows, squints and sneer or smirk move it down your face. On the cheek, tilt away from the candy so it doesn’t fall, and point your lips toward it to bring it to your mouth. Then open wide!

Keys: arrows tilt · space/B brows · Z/X squint · Q/W sneer · A/S smile · F frown · M mouth
